# CV presentation review

This review addresses presentation for AI engineering recruiters and master's admissions reviewers. It does not predict hiring or admission decisions. Existing CV content is the source of personal facts; metrics have not been independently audited.

## Findings and implemented changes

| Reviewer question | Previous obstacle | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is the candidate's focus? | Broad technology terms and many skill categories appeared before evidence. | More specific profile tagline; Skills follows experience, research, education, and projects. |
| What did the candidate contribute? | Impact was inside collapsed experience rows. | One visible contribution or scoped result for every role; full detail remains expandable. |
| What is the academic preparation? | Degree and GPA were far down the page; GPA required expansion. | Degree and GPA appear below About; GPA also appears in the closed Education entry. |
| What is the research contribution? | Long paper titles without an immediate study summary. | Visible author role, study scope/result, award tags, and a research-record summary with Google Scholar access. |
| Can I inspect the work? | Horizontal carousel hid projects; generic buttons obscured destinations. | All projects appear in a responsive grid; full descriptions, stack labels, descriptive accessible link names, and preserved direct app/repository links. |
| Can I trust the wording? | Unsupported 'state-of-the-art' language and ambiguous percentage notation. | Removed the unsupported claim, retained 'reported' qualifiers, wrote percentage-point units explicitly, and labeled GRPO as studying per the user's supplied experience. |
| Can I scan on a phone? | Organization names and roles were truncated. | Full titles wrap; dates stay on one line and summaries remain visible. |

The playful About voice, orange palette, official organization logos, solid borders, Lenis scrolling, and fixed interactive pet remain part of the site.

## Details still worth supplying

- Precise degree/major structure. Expected graduation is February 2027, confirmed by the owner and displayed in About and Education.
- Direct DOI, proceedings, or preprint links for each research work. Both HCLT 2026 handwriting papers are accepted for oral presentation, confirmed by the owner and labeled visibly; acceptance does not imply that the proceedings are already published. Google Scholar is a profile link, not a substitute for a paper-specific source.
- Evaluation protocol, baseline, held-out test size, and contribution ownership for reported results. Document counts are not represented as test-question counts; satisfaction is not represented as model accuracy.
- Dates for the BUFS research appointment and teaching assignment, plus public artifacts or supervisor details where appropriate.

These are credibility improvements requiring facts from the owner, not copywriting guesses. No missing details are shown as warnings to site visitors.

## Reference guidance

- Stanford Career Education, resume guidance: https://careered.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj22801/files/media/file/resume-and-cover-letter-examples.pdf
- Stanford Graduate Admissions, statements: https://gradadmissions.stanford.edu/apply/statements

The general principles are to make accomplishments inspectable and to connect preparation, research interests, and future direction. Individual employers and graduate programs use different criteria.
